What I Do

What I Do: I combine my education, experience, and my passion for problem solving with modern AI tools to create applications or automations to support small businesses and individual contributors.

Why I Do It: I developed a strong sense of customer / user empathy during my 10+ years working as a Senior Support Engineer for a large SaaS company. After multiple reorgs and policy changes, I came to the realization that I would no longer be able to provide the kind of service that I would like to provide working under my previous employer. I decided to start my own business so that I can cut through all of the corporate fluff, free myself from the pressure of stock prices and big company contracts, and target an audience that can benefit from the services that I offer.

Approach

Transparency

I provide full transparency up front. You will know what you're getting and what you're spending before making any commitments. I am not the type who looks to impress with industry buzzwords or well-sounding statements. My goal is to present solutions and improvements, not flex.

Honesty

The corporate world tends to dance around inconvenient truths as they aim to buy themselves time and avoid bad public relations. I didn't adhere to this when I worked under it and I'm not about to start now. Inconvenient truths are to be communicated and corrected, not danced around.

Reason & Empathy

The question of how much I can do takes priority over how much I can charge. I don't want to take your money unless I know that I can make you money. I want my services to be an investment, not a regret.

Relevant Experience

Process Improvement

Despite being hired as an Inventory Control Clerk at a packaging factory, I quickly adopted IT Service Desk responsibilities as I maintained and repaired printers, fax machines, copiers, user machines, servers, our telecommunication system, and our security system. During my tenure I was able to optimize our backup and recover procedures, recommend application changes that significantly reduced waste, and took part in restructuring productivity reports with a focus on union employee morale.

Customer Advoacy

In my role as a Senior Support Engineer, I stood firmly with the customer base as the company moved forward with a TLS 1.2 / SHA256 implementation strategy that caused a lot of issues for our customers and our Support teams. This was a year and a half long battle where I was able to save multiple customers significant time and money. Security upgrades were implemented the correct way after that period. After the fact, a search in our ticketing system revealed that we could have avoid over 5,000 trouble tickets had the company taken the customer concerns seriously.

Customer Empathy

I worked for a third party agency that offered phone services for a gas company. I received a call from a caller in Maryland who was having problems paying her bills. Her issue was that she continued to be employed and therefore she did not meet the mininum earning requirements to receive assistance. She had to choose between her utilitie bills and her medication. I printed out an envelope at work and then began reaching out to Churches both in my area and in the customer's. I was able to send her information that she could use for assistance as she continued to work.
