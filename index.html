What I Do

The Problem

The world is rapidly changing due to the rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Large companies have the budget and the resources necessary to implement automations and advanced software packages capable of increasing output and lowering expenses. Small businesses can greatly benefit from these modern technological advancements, but may not have the time or the budget to explore these benefits.

The Solution

I offer services tailored to helping small businesses understand and implement modern technology. I focus on necessity and efficiency, meaning that I attack the exact business problem that you wish to solve. Combining this approach alongside my business model allows me to offer services at a fair and reasonable price. As big companies implement emerging technologies, my goal is to ensure that small businesses are able to stay in the fight and even thrive.
