What I Do

I help small businesses and solopreneurs improve operations by creating affordable and efficient applications and automations. In my previous corporate role, I worked exclusively with larger businesses supporting a complex cloud-based software suite designed to streamline their business operations. Though, pricing made our software infeasible for smaller businesses. This creates an environment where larger companies have the resources to purchase powerful tools that allow their business to grow, where smaller businesses are stuck using older technology and performing a lot of manual work.

I feel that the AI / Automation boom is an opportunity to bridge the gap between what small businesses and large corporations can accomplish. A lot of small businesses are not implementing newer technologies due to a lack of time and energy to learn them and the associated financial burden. This is where I step in. I take an efficient, no-nonsense approach to streamlining business processes and solving problems. My goal isn't to see how much I can get from a client. My goal is to provide clients with the most efficient, intuitive, and effective tools possible at a fair and reasonable price.

Customer Advoacy

In my role as a Senior Support Engineer, I stood firmly with the customer base as the company moved forward with a TLS 1.2 / SHA256 implementation strategy that caused a lot of issues for our customers and our Support teams. This was a year and a half long battle where I was able to save multiple customers significant time and money. Security upgrades were implemented the correct way after that period. After the fact, a search in our ticketing system revealed that we could have avoid over 5,000 trouble tickets had the company taken the customer concerns seriously.

Customer Empathy

I worked for a third party agency that offered phone services for a gas company. I received a call from a caller in Maryland who was having problems paying her bills. Her issue was that she continued to be employed and therefore she did not meet the mininum earning requirements to receive assistance. She had to choose between her utilitie bills and her medication. I printed out an envelope at work and then began reaching out to Churches both in my area and in the customer's. I was able to send her information that she could use for assistance as she continued to work.
