About

I'm Ken, the founder of Empowering David, LLC. I help small businesses — the Davids — put modern technology to work where it removes redundancy, automates repetitive tasks, and saves time and money. Larger companies, the Goliaths, can buy the fancy equipment. David needed a sling and a stone. My goal is not to sell you a bronze helmet. It is to craft the sling and the stone that fit the fight.

Principles

  • What I can get from a client comes from what I can do for a client.

  • Clients will know exactly what they are getting before spending.
