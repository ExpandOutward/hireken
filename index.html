What I Do

I help small businesses and solopreneurs improve operations by creating affordable and efficient applications and automations. In my previous corporate role, I worked exclusively with larger businesses supporting a complex cloud-based software suite designed to streamline their business operations. Pricing made our software infeasible for smaller businesses. This creates an environment where larger companies have the resources to purchase powerful tools that allow their business to grow, where smaller businesses are stuck using older technology and performing a lot of manual work.

I feel that the AI / Automation boom is an opportunity to bridge the gap between what small businesses and large corporations can accomplish. A lot of small businesses are not implementing newer technologies due to a lack of time and energy to learn them and the associated financial burden. This is where I step in. I take an efficient, no-nonsense approach to streamlining business processes and solving problems. My goal isn't to see how much I can get from a client. My goal is to provide clients with the most efficient, intuitive, and effective tools possible at a fair and reasonable price.

What I Offer

Affordability

My business model was designed to keep costs low. I aim to accomplish as much as possible using as little as possible. I work out of my home office using equipment that I already own and my usage of AI allows me to perform the work of an entire team without adding headcount.

Transparency

You will know what you are getting up front before making any commitments.

Clarity

Different people learn different things in different ways. I take the human element into consideration when designing tools and documentation.

Honesty

Big corporations often hide inconvenient truths to avoid reputational damage. I feel that the act of hiding the truth warrants reputational damage in and of itself. If any issues arise during the end-to-end process I will be up front about them so that you know what is happening and can plan accordingly.
