About

I'm Ken, the founder of Empowering David, LLC. I feel that small businesses are an important part of American culture. Small businesses allow anyone to build something from the ground up, they drive competitive pricing, and they diverisify customer options. I want to see small businesses succeed in a world that sometimes seems to favor larger corporations.

Industrial Goliaths have the resources to implement solutions that accomplish their business goals but often come pre-packaged with unwanted features and functionalities. I focus on your exact business problem and build the technology from the ground up. I am not in the business of selling David a bazooka when all he needs is a sling, a stone, and a little bit of faith.

Principles

  1. The work will focus on accomplishing the business goal. Nothing more.
  2. Pricing is based on how much I can do for a client, not how much I can get from a client.
  3. Clients will know exactly what they are getting before spending.
